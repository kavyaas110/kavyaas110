### 🚀 About Me

I am a Full Stack Software Engineer with 3+ years of industry experience building and supporting large-scale, production systems serving millions of users. I recently completed my Master’s in Computer Science at Rochester Institute of Technology. My primary interests lie in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, with hands-on experience in conversational AI, speech technologies (STT/TTS), data analytics, and applied ML. I also bring a strong foundation in software engineering, system design, backend development, and building reliable production systems.  
I am particularly interested in the intersection of AI and software engineering, and in building responsible, scalable, and impactful technologies.
